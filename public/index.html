📖 Game Rules & Scoring Guides

⛳ Golf Card Game Rules

The goal is to obtain the lowest score possible over the course of 9 or 18 holes (rounds). Lowest total points at the end of the match wins!

Card Values:

Card Points Notes
Joker -2 Points The best card in the game (bonus)!
King (K) 0 Points Free zero! (In pairs, Kings cancel out each other as 0 pts)
Ace (A) 1 Point Standard low card
2 through 10 Face Value 2 = 2 pts, 3 = 3 pts ... 10 = 10 pts
Jack (J), Queen (Q) 10 Points High penalty face cards

Column Pair Cancellation Rule:

When two identical cards (e.g. two 8s, two 5s, or two Queens) are in the same vertical column, they form a pair and cancel out to 0 POINTS!
