📖 Golf Card Game Rules & Scoring

🏌️ Objective

Just like real golf, the goal is to obtain the lowest score possible over the course of 9 or 18 holes (rounds). Lowest total points at the end of the game wins!

🃏 Standard Card Values

Card Points Notes
Joker -2 Points The best card in the game!
King (K) 0 Points Free zero! (In pairs, Kings cancel out each other as 0 pts)
Ace (A) 1 Point Standard low card
2 through 10 Face Value 2 = 2 pts, 3 = 3 pts ... 10 = 10 pts
Jack (J), Queen (Q) 10 Points High penalty face cards

🎯 The Pairing / Column Cancellation Rule

When two identical cards (e.g., two 8s, two 5s, or two Queens) are placed in the same vertical column, they form a pair and cancel out to 0 POINTS!

Example: If you have a column with an 8 and an 8, that column scores 0 points instead of 16 points.

🎮 Variants Supported

  • 4-Card Golf: Players get 4 cards (2 rows of 2). Turn over 2 at start.
  • 6-Card Golf (Most Popular): Players get 6 cards (2 rows of 3). Turn over 2 at start.
  • 8-Card Golf: Players get 8 cards (2 rows of 4). Turn over 2 or 4 at start.
  • 9-Card Golf: Players get 9 cards (3 rows of 3). Turn over 3 at start; columns of 3 identical cards score 0 (or -10 depending on house rules).
