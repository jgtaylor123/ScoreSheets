📖 Game Rules & Scoring Guides

⛳ Golf Card Game Rules

The standard goal is to obtain the lowest score possible over the course of 9 or 18 holes (rounds). Lowest total points at the end of the match wins!

🎯 The Special 100-Point Rule:

If any player finishes the game with a total score of exactly 100 points, they instantly win the entire match outright! Otherwise, the lowest total score takes the victory.

Card Values:

Card Points Notes
2s -2 Points The best card in the game (bonus)!
King (K) 0 Points Free zero! (In pairs, Kings cancel out each other as 0 pts)
Ace (A) 1 Point Standard low card
3 through 10 Face Value 3 = 3 pts, 4 = 4 pts ... 10 = 10 pts
Jack (J), Queen (Q) 10 Points High penalty face cards

Column Pair Cancellation Rule:

When two identical cards (e.g. two 8s, two 5s, or two Queens) are in the same vertical column, they form a pair and cancel out to 0 POINTS!
